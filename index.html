How it works

  1. Load the official templates from the team's shared drive. The page reads them in your browser; the files never leave your computer.
  2. Fill in the details once. Shared fields such as project, client PM, modules and signatures go into both forms.
  3. Download both completed Word files, review them, then convert to PDF and send from your work email as usual.
